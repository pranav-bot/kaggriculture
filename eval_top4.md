## Definitions and assumptions

- **Seed-Matched Delta** = candidate terminal cash minus the deterministic surrogate terminal cash on the same replay seed.
- **Counterfactual Margin** = candidate terminal cash minus the recorded replay opponent's terminal cash. It is descriptive, not a claim that the recorded opponent can be reproduced exactly.
- **Divergence Rate** = turns requiring surrogate or safe-no-op fallback (including missing/malformed replay frames) divided by opponent turns.
- Replay actions use the observed `step + 1` offset documented in `research/recon_findings.md`; a PASS action is used after the replay.
- Terminal cash is farm money only; inventory and carried goods are not liquidated implicitly.

## Limitations and fixes

The evaluator does not mutate simulator code. If Kaggle dependencies are unavailable, an explicit error is emitted and no result is presented as valid. Malformed actions fall back deterministically and are counted; unexpected simulator exceptions fail that replay rather than silently inventing terminal cash.
